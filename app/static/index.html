Privacy Policy & Data Governance Specification

1. On-Device Execution Isolation

All algorithms, contour segmentations, and semantic projection routines execute exclusively in local CPU/GPU memory on the host computer. Zero telemetry, cookies, or tracking payloads are emitted.

2. Non-Hallucination Asset Provenance

In accordance with assignment requirements, image composition utilizes authenticated cutouts from existing catalog assets. Synthesized approximations and generative pixel fills are strictly disabled (generative_fill: false).

3. Cryptographic Manual Edit Protection

Manual annotations saved by users are quarantined from automated detector outputs. Automated re-runs cannot overwrite user edits without an explicit administrative force flag (ProtectedObjectsError).
